📊 Instagram Content & Growth Analytics Dashboard
Welcome to the Instagram Content & Growth Analytics Dashboard repository! This project features a comprehensive Power BI dashboard designed to track, analyze, and optimize Instagram engagement, reach, audience demographics, and content performance.

✨ Key Features & Dashboard Overview

📱 1. Instagram Overview
📈 Engagement Rate by Month: Visualizes monthly engagement trends across the year to identify peak interaction periods.   

🎯 Total Reach & Engagement KPIs: High-level metric cards tracking total engagement (662K), total followers (8K), and total reach (8M).   

📊 Content Breakdown: Comparative bar charts and donut charts analyzing comments, total reach, and profile visits broken down by content type (Photo, Reel, Carousel, and Video).   

🎛️ Campaign Tag Slicers: Interactive filters allowing users to slice data by campaign categories such as Giveaway, Launch, Promo, or None. 


🎬 2. Content Performance Analysis
👁️ Views & Engagement by Content Type: Horizontal rankings showing total views and engagement percentages for each post format.   

📌 Saves by Content Type: Visual tree maps analyzing user save behavior across formats.   

⏰ Views by Post Hour: Line charts highlighting optimal posting hours throughout the day to maximize audience reach (peaking near 1.01M views).

👥 3. Audience Demographics & Behavior
❤️ Likes & Shares by Content Type: Detailed bar charts measuring user appreciation and virality metrics per content category.   

🔗 Follow Clicks & Profile Visits: Tracking conversion funnels from content views to profile visits (46K) and follow clicks (10K).   

📅 Temporal Slicers: Multi-select month and content type filters for deep-dive filtering.
